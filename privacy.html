Privacy Policy

Last updated October 1, 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to WriteEase AI ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information and writing content are handled when you access and use our website and writing assistant tools.

Please note that WriteEase AI is currently operating as a demonstration and client-side static web application hosted on GitHub Pages. Because of this static architecture, our data handling practices are designed with privacy-first principles where your writing inputs remain directly under your control.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. The information collected depends on how you interact with WriteEase AI:

  • Account Information: When you register an account or create a profile, details such as your name, email address, and simulated authentication tokens are stored exclusively within your web browser's localStorage.
  • Writing and Tool Content: Any text you input into our tools (including the Paraphraser, AI Humanizer, Grammar Fixer, Email Writer, Templates, and Plagiarism Checker) is processed locally on your client device. In this demo version, no text or writing drafts are sent to or stored on remote backend servers.
  • Usage Statistics and History: Your tool run count, generated outputs, timestamped history records, and preferences are saved locally inside your browser storage.
  • Technical Information: Standard server logs maintained by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may collect basic non-identifiable technical data such as browser type, referring URL, and access timestamps.

3. How We Use Your Information

The limited information stored in your browser is utilized solely for the following purposes:

  • Providing and executing writing assistance tools (such as paraphrasing, grammar corrections, and email drafting).
  • Maintaining your local session state, tool history, and daily request quota.
  • Enabling you to revisit, review, copy, or export previously generated writing results from your local History page.
  • Improving user interface design, application usability, and overall client experience.

4. Data Storage and Security

Because WriteEase AI operates as a static client-side web application in demo mode:

  • All user profile information, writing drafts, and generated history reside entirely in your browser's localStorage on your local device.
  • No database servers or centralized cloud data stores receive or archive your private text submissions.
  • Because data resides on your personal device, you are responsible for maintaining the physical and digital security of your device and browser environment. Clearing your browser cache or site data will permanently remove all stored accounts and history.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We firmly respect the confidentiality of your writing. We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data or writing inputs to any third parties for marketing, advertising, or profiling purposes.

Furthermore, WriteEase AI does not integrate third-party ad tracking networks or telemetry surveillance scripts. Any external links (such as Google Fonts or external reference materials) are governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

WriteEase AI does not use traditional tracking cookies to monitor your browsing activities across other websites. Instead, we use HTML5 localStorage to manage your authentication state, tool run quotas, and user preferences locally.

You can manage, inspect, or delete this information at any time through your browser's developer tools or privacy settings.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Regardless of your geographic location, you retain full ownership and control over your data within WriteEase AI:

  • Access: You can view all saved texts and activity logs directly on the History and Dashboard pages.
  • Deletion: You may delete individual items from your History page, or wipe all account and stored data by clearing your browser storage or through the Settings panel.
  • Opt-Out: Since data is kept locally without remote telemetry, you can discontinue use at any time without leaving personal data on our servers.

8. Children's Privacy

WriteEase AI is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under 13 years of age. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please notify us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to address the issue.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product enhancements, architectural updates, or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:
