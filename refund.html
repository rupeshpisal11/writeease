Refund Policy

Last updated October 1, 2024

1. Overview

At WriteEase AI, we are committed to customer satisfaction and delivering a seamless writing experience. We stand behind the value of our tools and want you to feel confident in upgrading to our premium features. This Refund Policy describes the conditions, timelines, and procedures for obtaining a refund for paid subscriptions.

2. Free Plan

Our Free Plan provides 10 requests every day across all 6 core writing tools without requiring a credit card or billing details. Because no monetary charges are made for the Free Plan, no refund requests apply to free tier usage. We encourage all users to explore the Free Plan thoroughly before deciding to upgrade to Pro.

3. Pro Plan Refund Window

We provide a 7 calendar day money-back guarantee for new Pro Plan subscriptions. If you subscribe to a paid Pro tier and find that the platform does not meet your expectations, you may request a full refund within seven (7) calendar days from your initial transaction date.

4. Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a refund under our 7-day guarantee, your request must satisfy the following fair-use criteria:

  • The refund request is initiated within 7 calendar days from the original date and time of purchase.
  • Your account has not exceeded fifty (50) total Pro tool requests during the active billing cycle.
  • The refund request is for a first-time subscription purchase. Repeat subscriptions or renewals are not eligible for subsequent refunds.
  • Your account must be in good standing and not in breach of our Terms of Service (e.g., no abusive automated scraping or prohibited academic misconduct).

5. How to Request a Refund

Requesting a refund is simple and straightforward. To submit your request:

  • Send an email to hello@writeease.ai.
  • Use the subject line: Refund Request - [Your Order ID or Account Email].
  • Include your registered email address, date of purchase, and your transaction or order ID.
  • While not mandatory, we appreciate any brief feedback about why the service did not suit your needs so we can continue improving.

6. Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds will not be issued in the following circumstances:

  • Requests submitted after the 7-calendar-day refund window has elapsed.
  • Accounts that have exceeded the 50 Pro request fair-use threshold prior to submitting the refund request.
  • Subsequent renewal billing cycles where the user failed to cancel auto-renewal before the renewal date.
  • Accounts that have been suspended or terminated due to violations of our Acceptable Use policy or Terms of Service.

7. Processing Time and Payment Method

Once your refund request is received and verified by our support team, we will process your refund promptly. Approvals are typically reviewed within 2 business days. Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original payment method used at the time of purchase within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank or payment gateway provider.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require assistance concerning our Refund Policy or your subscription status, please reach out to us at:

Email: hello@writeease.ai
Website: writeease.ai
